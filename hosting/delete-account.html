🦉 Hapus Akun Beomora

Aplikasi Android "Beomora: Belajar Bahasa" (com.beomora.app) · English version below

Halaman ini menjelaskan cara menghapus akun Beomora beserta seluruh data yang terkait dengannya.

Cara 1 — Langsung dari aplikasi (instan)

  1. Buka aplikasi Beomora dan pastikan Anda sudah masuk (login).
  2. Buka tab Kampusku → Pengaturan.
  3. Ketuk Hapus Akun di bagian bawah.
  4. Konfirmasi dengan memilih akun Google Anda lagi, atau memasukkan sandi bila Anda mendaftar dengan email.

Akun dan data langsung terhapus permanen.

Cara 2 — Lewat email (tanpa membuka aplikasi)

Kirim email ke danilukman2206@gmail.com dari alamat email yang terdaftar di Beomora, dengan subjek "Hapus Akun Beomora". Permintaan diproses maksimal 7 hari kerja, dan kami akan membalas setelah selesai.

Data yang dihapus

  • Akun login (Firebase Authentication).
  • Profil: nama, alamat email, dan foto profil.
  • Nomor telepon.
  • Seluruh progres belajar, XP, streak, dan peringkat di Papan Juara.

Data yang disimpan

Beomora tidak menyimpan data apa pun milik Anda setelah akun dihapus. Rekaman audio dari Latihan Bicara tidak pernah disimpan maupun dikirim ke server kami. Catatan transaksi pembelian Premium dikelola oleh Google Play sesuai kebijakan Google, bukan oleh Beomora. Langganan yang masih aktif tidak otomatis berhenti, jadi batalkan lewat Play Store → Pembayaran & langganan.

🦉 Delete Your Beomora Account

Android app "Beomora: Belajar Bahasa" (com.beomora.app)

This page explains how to delete your Beomora account and all data associated with it.

Option 1: In the app (instant)

  1. Open Beomora and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to the My Campus tab → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom.
  4. Confirm by choosing your Google account again, or by entering your password if you signed up with email.

Your account and data are deleted permanently right away.

Option 2: By email (without the app)

Send an email to danilukman2206@gmail.com from the email address registered in Beomora, with the subject "Delete Beomora Account". Requests are processed within 7 business days, and we will reply once done.

Data that is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile: name, email address, and profile photo.
  • Phone number.
  • All learning progress, XP, streaks, and Leaderboard ranking.

Data that is kept

Beomora does not keep any of your data after the account is deleted. Speaking Practice audio is never stored or sent to our servers. Premium purchase records are handled by Google Play under Google's policies, not by Beomora. An active subscription does not stop automatically. Cancel it in Play Store → Payments & subscriptions.
